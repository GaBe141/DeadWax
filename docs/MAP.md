## The Reveal (design this first, serve it always)

Act 1 is spent believing you're descending a worn-out mining-town of a world. Then, at the bottom of the Overture, the Tonearm fight ends — by force, or with him *lowering you gently* — and you go through **the Drop**, crossing THE SCRATCH:

1. Long fall through the gouge. The camera pulls back for the *first time in the game*. The walls are groove-walls — the corridors you've been walking are the groove. The liner-book flips open mid-fall and sketches the helix: **"it's a record. this is the scratch. it's mine."**
2. Mid-fall, you panic-strike — and for the first time, **the air answers.** Below the Scratch nothing was ever played; decades of pressed sound pool thick enough to push against. The "double jump" arrives as the world changing, not a pickup. Nothing unlocked. Everything learned.
3. You land soft at the Landing — on freshly burnished wax that has no business being smooth (Hush's first unseen assist) — and the title card finally drops: **DEAD WAX.**

Triple reveal, ~8 seconds: geography (it's a record), mechanics (the air answers), identity (title card). Act 1's whole job is to earn it — and this time the wound you cross is *yours*.

**Map-shape rule:** the liner-book map must silhouette correctly — Act 1's explored rooms (paper cap + upper grooves) should look cylinder-ish in hindsight, but only resolve unmistakably when the Drop sketches the helix.

## The world-cylinder (groove winding down; ~59 rooms; crossable in ~2 min mastered)

---

### STRATUM 1 — THE LABEL (paper cap) — 11 rooms
*Printed-paper town; faded type; strikes die in spent wax. Everyone here has a worn name.*

- **The Headshell** — home/spawn: the Tonearm's cradle where Skip hung for decades. Quiet. The empty grip stays on screen all game.
- **The Horn Plaza** — the great horn, silent; the Hound beside it, one ear to the ground. Death returns you to cue points; the Hound does the carrying.
- **Rooms To Let** — Matron 33⅓'s boarding house: heal/save hub, sleeve-patch armor upgrades.
- **The Print Shop** — the Typesetter: shine → map pages, margin markers, letterpress trinkets.
- **The Practice Room** — Tick loops here ("3… 3… 3…"); the **COUNT-IN** refrain is chalked on the metronome beside them. The "key" refrain — opens groove-locks everywhere. Tick's own Rite comes much later.
- **The High Street** — the played mid-loop; parry tutorial vs. a Looper; the Postie stands mid-delivery at one slot; first "second-run door" (LIFT-skippable from minute one, taught never).
- **The Stalls** — dry market; strike-launch lessons on the only live plates up top; 2 secrets behind glass.
- **The Locked-Groove Yard** — the graveyard: one-bar loops, chalked worn names, faint listening spots, one Last-Bar fragment.
- Revisit reasons: Gather (Act 2) opens every dead-air balcony you stared at; deep-listening returns make the Yard speak; Spindle junction at the Headshell.

### STRATUM 2 — THE OVERTURE (the played grooves) — 8 rooms
*Worn thin, ghost-light. The wind still plays the most worn passages.*

- **The Bootlegger's Stall** — contraband tapes, refrain tabs, hint economy. Trades for blanks and shine.
- **Addie's Doorway** — *Adagio in C, 14:13–17:13.* The first mercy: the whole Rite loop taught in a doorway (her answer sleeps in the Yard above — the game's first reason to go BACK up).
- **The Whistlers** — galleries worn so thin the wind plays them; one-way sound-jets; foreshadowing: the world can be played, and the world can push you.
- **The Smoothed Floor** — **HUSH DUEL I.** An arena he has already burnished mirror-flat: no resonance, no echo-amp, your combat language muted by the room itself. He fights to take the lance. The fight gets quieter as it goes.
- **The Arm** — **B1: THE TONEARM**, at the bottom, directly above the wound. Force: beat the grip. Rite: play him her real words from the Yard's locked grooves — and his grip opens, and he *lowers you through the Scratch himself.* The gentlest drop in the game, as an act break.
- **THE DROP / THE SCRATCH** — the reveal (above). One-way on first pass; the Spindle reconnects it later.

### STRATUM 3 — THE UNPLAYED (below the wound) — 15 rooms, two lobes
*Never played. Thick with waiting sound: strike-jets live here. Both lobes open, either order.*

- **The Landing** — soft arrival on burnished wax; title card; Spindle junction II.
- **THE VERSE (lobe A)** — narrow, wordy, dense with meaning nobody heard. **The Deep Gallery** holds the Duet's partner's unplayed answer (SET only — it opens with "Vess"). **The Hand-Off** — **B2: THE DUET**, mandatory stealth: the arena is his hearing; move only inside his own sound.
- **THE CHORUS (lobe B)** — the hook: **the Hook Pools** loop trance-thick (soak warning in the liner-book, ignored nightly); **Walt's Ballroom** (micro-Rite → the step-turn dodge); Earworm territory; the **GATHER** refrain chalked at the pools' bottom — thickens air around you briefly: strike-jets *anywhere*, including the Label. The single biggest retraversal key.
- **The Mechanism** — stratum exit. **B3: THE CHANGER.** No grief, no Rite, no stealth: the resonance-combat exam, checking your hands before the deep half.
- Revisit: pool-drain states, Gather-locked alcoves, 3 secrets, one layer-3 mystery (drain the Hook Pools in the right order and they play a tab).

### STRATUM 4 — THE UNDERSONG (the deep arrangement) — 11 rooms
*Liquid unplayed sound; you swim. Hardest combat; richest listening. "soon." starts appearing on the walls down here — and at the stratum gate, THE WELCOME: Soon catches Skip mid-fall, holds him ten seconds, sets him down gently, and thanks him. Non-fight. Unforgettable.*

- **The Bassline** — swimming galleries; strike = jet propulsion; currents of undertow.
- **The Whisper Strata** — the lore payload: what each mourner lost, the Digger's runtime knowledge, the Engineer practicing — audible only in full stillness while things hunt. The **REST** refrain is learned here: hush the *world* briefly — freeze currents, calm the feral, pause hazards.
- **The Mispress** — a mispressed region of wrong grooves; hides the **JUMP-CUT** (layer-3 secret refrain: a deliberate skip that shatters resonant barriers and sequence-breaks hard; hurts a little to play. For the obsessed.)
- **The Quiet Gallery** — **HUSH DUEL II.** Burnished arena again, deeper stakes; "footwork. the deep is worse." was not a joke.
- **The Crates** — **B4: THE DIGGER.** Fights you from inside its hoard. Its Rite is chalked on a locked groove in the graveyard below it, in her hand.
- Revisit: Rest re-opens current-blocked galleries; 2 secrets; one Last-Bar fragment.

### STRATUM 5 — DEAD WAX (the runout) — 6 rooms
*Past the last note. Locked grooves, the bent Spindle, her signature. Short and dense — no filler this deep.*

- **The Green Room / point of no return** — SOON's place-body: a waiting room of coat checks and ticket stubs under a sign reading NOW SERVING: ∞. The point of no return is marked kindly, in plain scrawl, and you cross it by choosing to stop waiting. The Spindle is bent here — from the violence of the skip — and carries you no further.
- **The Cradle-Lathe** — where styli were cast. Where you are from. Where *he* is from. The quietest room in the game; one listening spot: your own casting-day.
- **The Locked Grooves** — the deep graveyard; the never-played dead; chalk in no hand you recognize (his).
- **The Runout** — **B5: THE UNHEARD, conducted by SOON** — everything you didn't play, pooled and pressed, wielded as the big bad's congregation and shield. Force: the hardest fight in the game, ending with Soon dying mid-almost. Rite: the whole descent audited (mercy starves the king), then **the cue** — her hand falls at last, and Soon releases, bows, and finishes its only sentence. **Hush arrives at the dropout — beside you if you gave him a reason, against you if you raced.**
- **The Etching** — her signature, and the last line before it.
- **The LAST BAR** — one input. The Run plays the world through the smoothed Scratch to here. The song finishes. The good kind of quiet.

## THE SPINDLE (fast-travel spine)

The world's axle: junctions at the Headshell, the Landing, and the Bassline — connect each on foot first (shortcut-loop law), then ride your own strike-wave along it. It is **bent** above Dead Wax and will not carry you into Act 3; the Crown of the world is walked, not ridden. It also quietly draws the map's centerline — the axis the whole song turns on.

## Gating chart (what actually blocks what)

| Gate type | Examples | Openable from minute one? |
|---|---|---|
| Skill | groove-stair zigzags, on-beat towers, B3 | yes — pure execution |
| Refrain (knowledge) | groove-locks, Gather alcoves, Rest currents | **yes, if you know the tab** |
| Body-knowledge | LIFT skips, listening spots, Auditioner mercies | **yes, always** |
| World-state | air density per stratum, pool-drain states | no — geography is honest |
| Story seal | the Tonearm at the Drop (B1), point of no return | no — the two hard doors |

Only two hard story doors in the whole game. Everything else honors the law: **the only gate is knowing.**

## Intended sequence breaks (features, not bugs)

- Know the Count-In tab? Skip the Practice Room entirely.
- Know the Gather? The Label's "unreachable" balconies are yours in Act 1.
- Learned LIFT-discipline from a friend/wiki/last run? Ghost the High Street from room one; the Duet becomes a puzzle you already solved.
- Jump-Cut carried by a second-run player: shatters the Whistlers shortcut, cuts ~20 min. Speedrun route: Headshell → Stalls balcony (Gather) → the Drop → Chorus skip → the Mechanism → Bassline → Runout. Target sub-40.

## Second-visit economy (the density audit)

59 rooms × ~2 meaningful visits ≈ 120 room-visits ≈ 3h at ~90s/visit. Numbers hold from v1. Trim line: trim the Chorus first, then one Bassline gallery; **never trim the Label** — the reveal needs the player to have *lived* up there, and the ending needs somewhere worth carrying the song back to.

## What this doc doesn't decide

Room-by-room layouts (graybox per stratum, feel-first), enemy placement, exact tab patterns, Last-Bar fragment locations (design alongside Whisper Strata content), the exact staging of Hush's three assists (Drop-landing is assist #1; the other two live in Act 2), and whether the epilogue Run is playable at full length or cut-scene-assisted (strong candidate: playable, riding the Spindle's repaired line during credits).
