DEAD WAX — character style comparison

the same kid, four ways. pick with your eyes; the verdicts are my read on what each one costs us.
My recommendation up front: the scratchy hand-drawn boil (column 3) — it's the one style that's native to mechanics we already built: boil-is-state (a soul's framerate tells you if it's freed/stuck/machine), the scratchboard below-Scratch world, and the two-world ink trick (the same line just recolors, black-on-paper above → white-on-black below). Pixel isn't just a different look — it quietly breaks four things (see the table). Sketchbook-rough (column 4) is already canon, but only for Soon. Cartoon-clean (column 2) is gorgeous and wrong: it looks like a 2024 mobile game, not a 2006 Newgrounds fever dream.

1 · Pixel

SNES-indie retro · chunky
+ cheap frames, rock-solid at 16px, stable pipeline.
− boil reads as a "broken sprite," not a trembling soul. needs 2 full palettes for the two worlds. no line-character for scratchboard. fights the whole Newgrounds premise.

2 · Cartoon-clean

Flash-tween · polished vector
+ crisp, readable, "expensive"-looking.
− reads as a modern mobile/console indie. loses the 2006-scrappy soul. hardest to keep consistent frame-to-frame. the "feral kid at 2am" is gone.

3 · Scratchy boil

the house style · hand-drawn, 2-frame boil
+ boil IS the mechanic (4f freed / 2f stuck / 0f machine). scratchboard-native. one line, two worlds. cheap BECAUSE it's meant to look rough. it IS the 2006 aesthetic.
− needs silhouette discipline so tells stay readable in hard combat (solvable — that's what the 16px rule is for).

4 · Sketchbook-rough

construction lines showing · Soon's look
+ perfect for Soon (unfinished-on-purpose; hides in wall hatching). raw, alive.
− too noisy for the whole cast. hurts instant readability — bad for a hard combat game where every tell must land in a frame. keep it as Soon's signature, not the house.

The honest read: this isn't really "cartoon vs pixel" — it's "do we keep the four mechanics we designed around a drawn line, or trade them for pixel's cheaper frames." My vote is column 3 as the house style, column 4 reserved for Soon, a whisper of column 2's polish only on the paper-world UI chrome, and pixel set aside — because pixel would make us re-invent boil-is-state and the scratchboard world from scratch. But it's your call: if you want pixel badly enough, we re-plan those mechanics and I'll draw you a pixel bible instead. Say the word.
