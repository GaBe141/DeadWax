# Dead Wax agent notes

## Project

- This is a Godot 4.7 2D game with 21 authored rooms across the Label, Overture, Scratch, and first Unplayed loop, using the GL Compatibility renderer. The remaining planned world is development scaffolding.
- `scenes/main.tscn` is the entry point. `scripts/main.gd` creates the input map, player, camera, HUD, audio, menus, and rooms at runtime, so an empty editor viewport is expected. Normal boot opens the title screen; New Game plays the opening before beginning in the Headshell and Continue restores the saved room entry directly.
- Existing audio is synthesized in `scripts/audio_bank.gd`; there are no external audio assets or third-party dependencies.
- `scripts/press.gd` owns the entire visual language — plates, stock, the paper sheet, the type case, signage cards, and architectural impressions through `scripts/press_impression.gd`. Rooms author `ink` and `bg_color`; anything that needs a new surface or a new size of type adds it to the press rather than styling in place. The press reads no gameplay state: colours arrive as arguments.
- The 21 authored rooms opt into `room_atmosphere.gd` after building platforms. Its still `Air` shader sits beneath static far artwork and selected moving middle details; camera travel gives those two planes parallax. `Press.draw_room_depth` dispatches pure drawing to `press_label_depth.gd`, `press_overture_depth.gd`, and `press_unplayed_depth.gd`. Foreground engraving is clipped to actual platform faces at least 10 px below walkable tops. Decoration must never add collision, cover landing edges, bridge gaps, or change routes/rewards. Development rooms and grayboxes retain the original halftone backdrop.
- Atmosphere receives palette, camera position, reduced-motion settings, and encounter outcomes explicitly. Pause freezes it; reduced motion freezes decorative ambient movement and parallax while still allowing palette/outcome updates. Reink every layer on A/B changes without mutating authored room colours. Keep static far artwork cached, redraw moving middle details only as needed, and synchronize parallax immediately after recovery.
- `room_lighting.gd` is a sibling of Atmosphere. `lighting_profiles.gd` authors two to four native `PointLight2D` sources per campaign room; Press supplies their shared radial texture and fixture drawing. Each active authored room owns one `CanvasModulate`; Main removes the old room before adding its replacement. Lights stay fixed in world space, independent of parallax, and affect canvas layer 0 only so the sheet, HUD, and menus retain their colours. Development rooms and grayboxes create no Lighting node.
- Lighting occluders use actual platform rectangles with `grow(-2.0)`; they add no physics or gameplay state. Pause freezes lighting; reduced motion stops slow modulation while live encounter outcomes can still update energy. B-side reinking recognizes an actual swap of the initial authored ink/stock and adds ambient fill; do not infer the side from brightness because the painted A-side already has light ink. Returning to A restores authored exposure. Preserve shaded world materials and the GL Compatibility renderer.
- Animated figures draw through Press and the `press_skip`, `press_auditioner`, `press_pressing`, `press_hush`, `press_tonearm`, `press_resident`, and `press_hound` helpers. Pose dictionaries and palettes are explicit inputs. Presentation clocks/impulses must never drive combat timers or scale/rotate the gameplay node or its collider. Main calls Skip's `reset_animation()` on recovery and passages; restored outcomes start settled, without replaying rewards. The tree pause freezes animation.
- `resident.gd` gives Tick and the Bootlegger grounded E/Y conversations and harmless routines; `hound.gd` patrols within authored bounds and responds to still, quiet Hood company. They only read injected `session_outcomes`, add no collision or persistent encounter metadata, and never grant progression or Shine. Freed Addie walks home and putters within 55px of her authored doorway; her hood-pat is presentation only, and shattered Addie remains gone. Existing Count-In and encounter rules still own every outcome.
- Only `groove_yard/yard_first_voice` uses `yard_voice.gd`, an Auditioner subclass. A complete two-note Hood call (0.98s) precedes a fresh held Set in a 2s silence; one 0.4s answer frees it. Input is sampled alongside Skip in physics; the silence opens as the second note ends. Early/held Set never bypasses the call. Quiet exchanges suspend its reach, misses retry, and leaving or striking cancels the attempt. Ordinary strike/parry/pogo values remain inherited; the second Yard voice stays ordinary. Its existing stable freed/shattered key still belongs to Main. Restoring removes resolved actors silently as before.
- `yard_memory.gd` presents that outcome at fixed (1110,587), with no collision, encounter metadata, progression, or rewards. `press_yard_voice.gd` draws the actor cues and completed/fractured engraving through Press. Live outcomes reveal the engraving; restored ones start settled. A freed engraving recalls a finite synthesized phrase only after six quiet seconds nearby, with nine seconds between starts; pause freezes it. Reduced motion freezes decoration while call/answer cues and memory scheduling remain active. Main's home melody still belongs exclusively to the loft outcome.
- `assets/art/` contains four original generated production paintings for the Label, Overture halls, Well, and Unplayed, with art direction and prompts in `ART_DIRECTION.md`. `press_painted_world.gd` caches and crops these into the far plane; it never reads gameplay. `press_world_brush.gd` and `figure_paint.gd` supply pure drawn material/character detail. Keep raster originals and `.import` settings together. `assets/fonts/` contains Big Shoulders and IBM Plex Mono; both are SIL OFL and their licences must remain beside them. Audio remains fully synthesized.
- Shaders must stay GL Compatibility-safe: canvas shaders with UV/FRAGCOORD maths only, no `hint_screen_texture` or backbuffer reads. Plates carry ink, stock, and accent as shader parameters rather than a flat fill, so tests read colour via `get_shader_parameter`, not `ColorRect.color`.
- `data/world_map.json` version 2 separates notable route `kind`, progression `requires`, and `direction`. Keep planned rooms non-overlapping and the contact/special graph connected; routing health is locked by smoke tests.
- `scripts/world_map.gd` is the runtime reader for that plan: topology and presentation only, no gameplay. Its contact resolution mirrors the model the smoke suite locks — a declared special replaces the open contact for that pair, and a one-way special is never walked back from its target.
- `scripts/room_graybox.gd` builds a traversable shell for any planned room: 600x360 px per grid cell, stratum palette and air, one passage per planned route, one `from_<room>` arrival per inbound route. Climb rungs are sized under `SkipScript.JUMP_VELOCITY`'s rise so no graybox needs a Refrain to cross itself; keep that property when tuning either side.
- Main separates the authored campaign from two opt-in development atlases. Normal `_load_world_room(id)` uses `scripts/campaign.gd`, combining `chapter_one.gd`/`room_opening.gd`, `chapter_two.gd`/`room_overture.gd`, and `chapter_three.gd`/`room_unplayed_campaign.gd`, and refuses ids outside its 21-room registry. With `-- --dev-rooms`, `_load_room(index)` keeps the five prototype rooms and `_load_world_room(id)` creates planned grayboxes. `world_room_id` is non-empty for either an authored room or a planned graybox. Planned ids must never shadow a prototype id; authored ids intentionally match the plan without replacing its development shells.
- Campaign rooms expose `objective_label`. The Arm creates its final `chapter_marker.gd` only after the Tonearm is freed or shattered; grounded passage input emits `chapter_completed`. Entering the room or resolving the boss alone must never show an ending. Overture Stair now continues to the Bootlegger. Keep exits within the authored registry.
- Either Tonearm resolution also opens the Arm's separate eastern passage into `the_drop`. Chapter three authors `the_drop`, `the_landing`, `verse_hall`, `verse_warren_n`, `verse_warren_s`, and `deep_gallery`. The Drop↔Landing↔Verse Hall approach joins the North↔Gallery↔South↔North loop. Every new route returns on ordinary jumps, including the Drop's 100 px maintenance steps. The Landing's 190 px Gather overlook is optional; its Surveyor's Slip retains a clue to the Stalls loft and grants no ability or Shine. Passage use requires neither Gather nor the chapter `completed` flag and never triggers the Arm ending. Keep the planned 53-room atlas and its one-way Arm→Drop special unchanged.
- `room_unplayed_campaign.gd` uses inherited Auditioner and Test Pressing combat rules with stable campaign encounter IDs. Main resets unfinished `reset_on_recovery` attempts; saved resolutions retire actors silently. Its fixed `listening_post.gd` instances only advance grounded E/Y text and own no save, reward, or progression state. The expansion adds no Shine source or Refrain pickup; the nine patches still fund the three purchases. ## Regression-sensitive behavior

- The muted dummy never builds resonance. Its third successful parry emits `bout_won` without also emitting `shattered`.
- High Street's persistent encounter uses `street_looper.gd`, a Test Pressing subclass; the Deep Gallery trial also creates temporary derived copies without campaign identity. Its first unguarded hit starts an uninterrupted three-tick count and fourth-beat swing; guard strikes cannot damage, resonate, rebound, or rewind the count. Miss, hit, and parry each leave a one-second opening. HP, hit reach, and the 100 ms parry remain inherited. Main resets unfinished `reset_on_recovery` encounters; resolved `high_street/street_looper=shattered` stays silent with no new reward. Press draws its guard/open cues from an explicit snapshot.
- Player pogo reach intentionally matches enemy strike-hit reach at 120 px, so every enemy rebound is also a confirmed hit. Grounded foe strikes preserve footing; airborne strikes, including a jump in the same physics tick, can rebound from a vulnerable foe. Muted Test Pressings/HUSH are not pogoable from raw hits. Grooves retain launch priority on the ground and in the air.
- Strike cooldown remains 200 ms and the parry window remains 100 ms. One fresh press in the final 90 ms of cooldown may queue a strike; holding does not repeat. Stamp the parry clock when the strike executes. Hood, Set, damage, menus, respawn, and room transitions clear pending strikes. Ground recovery lasts 100 ms at 80% movement acceleration.
- Skip owns a three-strike Tap/Sweep/Accent chain with a 650 ms window between executed strikes. Queued input never advances it; public strike cancellation also clears the chain. Accent sets the existing `big` hit flag after launch resolution, so it neither stacks with a hot groove nor changes movement, reach, breath refill, or parry timing. `_strike_combo` is a separate captured presentation input; animation never drives combo state. `combo_readout.gd` only renders Main-supplied snapshots, with no independent gameplay clock.
- Combo snapshots also expose recovery, available input, queued input, and blocked state independently of link time. Fresh presses before the final 90 ms are explicitly rejected; accepted presses survive the physics tick crossing cooldown zero. Main forwards `strike_input_rejected` to a short readout receipt. Its lifetime pauses with play and expires under Reduced motion; accepted input, cancellation, and recovery clear stale feedback.
- Title's Move practice creates `room_move_practice.gd`, an empty floor with side walls and no campaign/atlas registration. Main's `practice_mode` swaps in disposable progression, economy, map, discoveries, collection, pressing, and encounter models while keeping `_has_session` false. Exiting restores the original model objects before Continue/New Game; practice cannot save, record visits, or route into the campaign.
