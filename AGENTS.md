# Dead Wax agent notes

## Project

- This is a Godot 4.7 2D game with fifteen authored rooms across the Label and Overture, using the GL Compatibility renderer. The remaining planned world is development scaffolding.
- `scenes/main.tscn` is the entry point. `scripts/main.gd` creates the input map, player, camera, HUD, audio, menus, and rooms at runtime, so an empty editor viewport is expected. Normal boot opens the title screen; New Game plays the opening before beginning in the Headshell and Continue restores the saved room entry directly.
- Existing audio is synthesized in `scripts/audio_bank.gd`; there are no external audio assets or third-party dependencies.
- `scripts/press.gd` owns the entire visual language — plates, stock, the paper sheet, the type case, signage cards, and architectural impressions through `scripts/press_impression.gd`. Rooms author `ink` and `bg_color`; anything that needs a new surface or a new size of type adds it to the press rather than styling in place. The press reads no gameplay state: colours arrive as arguments.
- The fifteen authored rooms opt into `room_atmosphere.gd` after building platforms. Its still `Air` shader sits beneath static far artwork and selected moving middle details; camera travel gives those two planes parallax. `Press.draw_room_depth` dispatches pure drawing to `press_label_depth.gd` and `press_overture_depth.gd`. Foreground engraving is clipped to actual platform faces at least 10 px below walkable tops. Decoration must never add collision, cover landing edges, bridge gaps, or change routes/rewards. Development rooms and grayboxes retain the original halftone backdrop.
- Atmosphere receives palette, camera position, reduced-motion settings, and encounter outcomes explicitly. Pause freezes it; reduced motion freezes decorative ambient movement and parallax while still allowing palette/outcome updates. Reink every layer on A/B changes without mutating authored room colours. Keep static far artwork cached, redraw moving middle details only as needed, and synchronize parallax immediately after recovery.
- `room_lighting.gd` is a sibling of Atmosphere. `lighting_profiles.gd` authors two to four native `PointLight2D` sources per campaign room; Press supplies their shared radial texture and fixture drawing. Each active authored room owns one `CanvasModulate`; Main removes the old room before adding its replacement. Lights stay fixed in world space, independent of parallax, and affect canvas layer 0 only so the sheet, HUD, and menus retain their colours. Development rooms and grayboxes create no Lighting node.
- Lighting occluders use actual platform rectangles with `grow(-2.0)`; they add no physics or gameplay state. Pause freezes lighting; reduced motion stops slow modulation while live encounter outcomes can still update energy. B-side reinking adds ambient fill for white ink, and returning to A restores authored exposure. Preserve shaded world materials and the GL Compatibility renderer.
- Animated figures draw through Press and the `press_skip`, `press_auditioner`, `press_pressing`, `press_hush`, `press_tonearm`, `press_resident`, and `press_hound` helpers. Pose dictionaries and palettes are explicit inputs. Presentation clocks/impulses must never drive combat timers or scale/rotate the gameplay node or its collider. Main calls Skip's `reset_animation()` on recovery and passages; restored outcomes start settled, without replaying rewards. The tree pause freezes animation.
- `resident.gd` gives Tick and the Bootlegger grounded E/Y conversations and harmless routines; `hound.gd` patrols within authored bounds and responds to still, quiet Hood company. They only read injected `session_outcomes`, add no collision or persistent encounter metadata, and never grant progression or Shine. Freed Addie walks home and putters within 55px of her authored doorway; her hood-pat is presentation only, and shattered Addie remains gone. Existing Count-In and encounter rules still own every outcome.
- Only `groove_yard/yard_first_voice` uses `yard_voice.gd`, an Auditioner subclass. A complete two-note Hood call (0.98s) precedes a fresh held Set in a 2s silence; one 0.4s answer frees it. Input is sampled alongside Skip in physics; the silence opens as the second note ends. Early/held Set never bypasses the call. Quiet exchanges suspend its reach, misses retry, and leaving or striking cancels the attempt. Ordinary strike/parry/pogo values remain inherited; the second Yard voice stays ordinary. Its existing stable freed/shattered key still belongs to Main. Restoring removes resolved actors silently as before.
- `yard_memory.gd` presents that outcome at fixed (1110,587), with no collision, encounter metadata, progression, or rewards. `press_yard_voice.gd` draws the actor cues and completed/fractured engraving through Press. Live outcomes reveal the engraving; restored ones start settled. A freed engraving recalls a finite synthesized phrase only after six quiet seconds nearby, with nine seconds between starts; pause freezes it. Reduced motion freezes decoration while call/answer cues and memory scheduling remain active. Main's home melody still belongs exclusively to the loft outcome.
- `assets/fonts/` (Big Shoulders for display, IBM Plex Mono for body) and `assets/shaders/` are the project's only non-code assets. Both fonts are SIL OFL and their licences ship beside them; keep those files together. Audio remains fully synthesized.
- Shaders must stay GL Compatibility-safe: canvas shaders with UV/FRAGCOORD maths only, no `hint_screen_texture` or backbuffer reads. Plates carry ink, stock, and accent as shader parameters rather than a flat fill, so tests read colour via `get_shader_parameter`, not `ColorRect.color`.
- `data/world_map.json` version 2 separates notable route `kind`, progression `requires`, and `direction`. Keep planned rooms non-overlapping and the contact/special graph connected; routing health is locked by smoke tests.
- `scripts/world_map.gd` is the runtime reader for that plan: topology and presentation only, no gameplay. Its contact resolution mirrors the model the smoke suite locks — a declared special replaces the open contact for that pair, and a one-way special is never walked back from its target.
- `scripts/room_graybox.gd` builds a traversable shell for any planned room: 600x360 px per grid cell, stratum palette and air, one passage per planned route, one `from_<room>` arrival per inbound route. Climb rungs are sized under `SkipScript.JUMP_VELOCITY`'s rise so no graybox needs a Refrain to cross itself; keep that property when tuning either side.
- Main separates the authored campaign from two opt-in development atlases. Normal `_load_world_room(id)` uses `scripts/campaign.gd`, combining `chapter_one.gd`/`room_opening.gd` and `chapter_two.gd`/`room_overture.gd`, and refuses ids outside its fifteen-room registry. With `-- --dev-rooms`, `_load_room(index)` keeps the five prototype rooms and `_load_world_room(id)` creates planned grayboxes. `world_room_id` is non-empty for either an authored room or a planned graybox. Planned ids must never shadow a prototype id; authored ids intentionally match the plan without replacing its development shells.
- Campaign rooms expose `objective_label`. The Arm creates its final `chapter_marker.gd` only after the Tonearm is freed or shattered; grounded passage input emits `chapter_completed`. Entering the room or resolving the boss alone must never show an ending. Overture Stair now continues to the Bootlegger. Keep exits within the authored registry.
- Refrains are the only reward a graybox grants, and they sit on a plinth clear of the spawn and of every arrival anchor. A pickup within `COLLECT_RADIUS` of an arrival would hand over a permission for merely entering and unseal the shortcuts leading there.
- `scripts/progression_state.gd` is a Main-owned in-memory model injected into rooms and the player. Main snapshots it into campaign saves through `scripts/save_store.gd`; the model itself does no disk I/O and needs no Autoload. Development progression remains session-only and must never overwrite the campaign checkpoint.
- Main owns disk integration and encounter outcomes. Chapter entities carry stable `chapter_state_id` metadata, persisted under `room_id/encounter_id`; `restore_encounters` silently reapplies opened doors, resolved encounters, and polished wax. Restoring must never emit rewards, discover knowledge, or mint Shine again.
- Main injects `session_outcomes` before room construction. HUSH's `smoothed_floor/hush=won` opens his forward passage; reaching the Arm opens `the_arm/gallery_shortcut=opened`. Outcome exits never grant Refrains or knowledge. Unfinished `chapter_boss` encounters reset on recovery; resolved ones remain silent. The Tonearm never attacks until struck, and both `freed` and `shattered` open the final listening point and change the Headshell return.
- Version-1 saves from the eight-room demo remain valid. `campaign.gd` treats `completed` as current completion only when a resolved `the_arm/tonearm` outcome is also present; Continue preserves the old room, entry, Shine, progression, and choices while clearing the obsolete ending flag.
- `scripts/save_store.gd` validates the versioned `user://deadwax-save.json` checkpoint, stages complete writes, and keeps a validated `.bak` recovery copy. Main saves on passages, locks, outcomes, polishing, purchases, the Book, pause, title, and quit. Continue and recovery use the saved room entry with full needle health (three hits by default, four with Spare Groove) while preserving progression and outcomes.
- `scripts/game_menu.gd` presents title, pause, settings, and the chapter ending and emits intent. Main owns pausing, saves, settings application, and transitions. Settings live in `user://deadwax-settings.cfg`: volume, fullscreen, and reduced camera motion. Reduced motion disables camera smoothing, shake, decorative ambient motion, and scenery parallax.
- `opening_cutscene.gd` presents four shots (23 seconds), captions, Next, and Skip on an ALWAYS canvas layer. `press_opening.gd` draws its pure print artwork through Press; reduced motion holds composed stills while the timeline continues. Main saves a new Headshell entry before playing, owns pause and deferred input cleanup, and prevents other menus/passages from opening. Title's Watch opening returns to title without touching the checkpoint. Continue and development skip it; `_new_game(false)` is the explicit fixture bypass. No viewed flag or save-schema change is needed. Opening audio uses one ALWAYS player with four lazy synthesized cues, independent of paused world audio; skip/cancel clears playback and master volume still applies.
- Core verbs are never permission-gated. Knowledge techniques are journal/discovery state only. Earned Refrains may alter traversal; Gather derives an effective breath capacity with `max(room capacity, 1)` and must not mutate room `air_density`.
- The authored campaign now offers Gather after either Tonearm resolution: a fixed-origin pickup at (1465,554) plus an optional 190px practice ledge. Neither auto-grants progression nor gates the explicit ending. Old completed saves can return for it. Dry Gather requires an airborne strike (same-frame jump counts) and lifts upward while the player steers; grounded combat remains planted, while environmental thick air keeps directional jets and live grooves keep their original launches.
- The Stalls loft is an optional 200px ascent reachable with Gather. `loft_voice.gd` asks for two complete Hood calls followed by fresh held Set responses in the silent windows; its visible cues remain active with Reduced motion. Main persists `the_stalls/loft_voice=freed`, opening both ends of the Stalls↔Gallery passage without Shine or knowledge rewards. The carried chart has seventeen real passage pairs.
- `audio_bank.gd` synthesizes the matching lost phrase and a gentle home loop. Main enables that loop only in the Horn Plaza/Headshell after the saved loft voice outcome. Pause suspends playback; transitions fade it, Continue restores it, and New Game clears it. Audio requests are idempotent and do not restart a paused phrase.
- A live earned shelf appends its real surface to atmosphere/lighting through `refresh_atmosphere`; existing layers, clocks, and lamps stay stable. Fixed-origin Refrain pickup and loft artwork draw through `press_discovery.gd` via Press. Room motion settings propagate to direct child presentations.
- `scripts/pressing_state.gd` is a Main-owned, session-only model of which side of the wax is face-up and how much of that side is left. Rooms author their A-side only; the B-side is the same room re-presented, never a duplicate. On the A-side every derived value must equal the room's authored value, so nothing changes for a player who never flips. Turning over must not mutate a room's authored `air_density`, `bg_color`, or `ink`.
- Flipping is gated by the Jump-Cut Refrain and is silent without it — an unearned Refrain is never announced before it is found. A groove carries a `side` and leaves the `live_groove` group entirely when its face is down, so no strike path has to re-check sides. `muted` is one-sided: HUSH only burnished the face that was up.
- `scripts/room_exit.gd` presents passages, and rooms forward semantic target/entry IDs through `room_base.gd`. `main.gd` alone performs deferred transitions and stores the active entry for R/death respawns. Preserve that ownership boundary.
- `scripts/inventory_menu.gd` is a full-screen, read-only view over Main's progression/player state. It may pause the tree but must never own or mutate progression, Shine, rooms, or saves. Refresh on open because snapshot restoration intentionally emits no signals.
- `economy_state.gd` is Main's in-memory Shine wallet and permanent purchase catalog. Skip's `shine` property delegates to it; `add_shine()` credits and emits presentation feedback. Each authored polish patch still pays exactly one Shine once. Do not add encounter rewards or reset polished patches to create income. The nine patches fund the three optional purchases (4/3/2 Shine).
- The Bootlegger's shop opens with grounded nearby B/D-pad Up. E/Y retains dialogue. `shop_menu.gd` only presents a snapshot and emits purchase/close intent; Main pauses play, validates context, debits the wallet and saves purchases together with Shine, rolling back the transaction if saving fails. Benefits apply only after a successful write. Preserve close-input suppression and shop/Book/pause/transition exclusion.
- Version-1 saves now accept optional `purchases`, an array of unique catalog IDs; absent means empty. Never change an old save's balance while adding the field. New Game resets wallet and purchases; Continue and recovery reapply purchased health, Hood speed, and trim. Purchases cannot grant core verbs, Refrains, or knowledge and cannot gate routes.

- `ui_motion.gd` owns cancellable interface impressions and noninteractive button accents. Menus inherit ALWAYS processing so they animate during pause; their opaque backing, hitboxes, focus, and semantic actions never wait on animation. Close and resize settle unfinished motion. Main propagates reduced motion to every menu and the HUD immediately.
- `hud_motion.gd` only animates supplied noninteractive type and receipts. Main owns the values/events; HUD clocks pause with gameplay. Recovering or entering a room clears transient receipts; silent state restoration never plays a Shine reward. Reduced motion settles transforms while notification lifetimes still expire.

- `map_state.gd` is Main-owned carried-map ownership and visited-room state; it grants no core verbs, Refrains, Shine, or traversal. The optional v1 root `map` field is strictly validated; absent means unowned with no visit history. New Game resets it, Continue restores before room construction, and campaign entries record visits even before pickup. Development atlases never record visits.
- Headshell FoldedMap sits at (500,554), clear of arrivals, with fixed 52px collection reach. Its draw bob never moves its node. Main connects room.map_collected before adding the room, applies ownership once, and queues the checkpoint. Owned pickups retire silently. Pause/reduced motion and A/B reinking apply to its presentation.
- `campaign_chart.gd` contains only the fifteen authored-room labels, schematic positions, and real undirected passage pairs. Keep it independent of room factories: saves preload it through map_state. `map_menu.gd` presents a snapshot with current room and visited names, never teleports or unlocks a route. Main owns pause, menu exclusion, and deferred close-input suppression. M/D-pad Down opens it in play; Book retains D-pad navigation and opens via its button or keyboard M.

## Work and verification

- Open this directory as a Godot project and press F5 for the opening chapter, or run `.\deadwax.cmd play`. `.\deadwax.cmd dev` opts into the original mechanics rooms and world tools, equivalent to Godot's `-- --dev-rooms` user argument.
- Before handing off code changes, run `.\deadwax.cmd check`. It imports resources and runs twenty-four dependency-free native suites: `tests/smoke_test.gd`, `tests/save_store_test.gd`, `tests/campaign_test.gd`, `tests/tonearm_test.gd`, `tests/overture_test.gd`, `tests/sprite_animation_test.gd`, `tests/residents_test.gd`, `tests/economy_state_test.gd`, `tests/economy_test.gd`, `tests/scenery_test.gd`, `tests/lighting_test.gd`, `tests/attack_feel_test.gd`, `tests/gui_animation_test.gd`, `tests/map_item_test.gd`, `tests/gather_reward_test.gd`, `tests/gather_route_test.gd`, `tests/loft_voice_test.gd`, `tests/home_song_test.gd`, `tests/opening_cutscene_test.gd`, `tests/opening_audio_test.gd`, `tests/yard_voice_test.gd`, `tests/yard_audio_test.gd`, `tests/combo_test.gd`, and `tests/practice_room_test.gd`.
- The suites cover existing mechanics and world topology, validated saves and recovery, and chapter rooms, passages, menus, and persistence. After gameplay changes, also follow `PLAYTEST.md`; timing, audio, rendering, and controller feel remain manual.
- There is no `export_presets.cfg`; this repository is not yet configured for distributable builds.
- Runtime controls are defined in `scripts/main.gd`: A/D or arrows move, Space jumps, J/X strikes, hold K/C raises the Hood, hold L kneels/Sets, F/right shoulder flips the pressing, E/gamepad Y enters passages or listens, B/D-pad Up browses the nearby Bootlegger, I/gamepad Start opens The Book, M/D-pad Down opens the collected map while exploring, and R recovers at the active entry. Escape/gamepad Back pauses campaign play; Escape closes the Book or stall first. Back remains respawn in development mode. In development mode, Tab cycles rooms, M toggles the planned world, and G grants every Refrain; that atlas behavior requires both a debug build and explicit development mode.
- Follow the existing GDScript conventions: tabs, `snake_case` names, typed signatures and variables, and `UPPER_SNAKE_CASE` constants. Preserve `.gd.uid` sidecars.
- Keep gameplay tuning in the constants already grouped near the tops of the relevant scripts.
- Preserve the GL Compatibility renderer unless a task specifically requires a renderer change.

## Handoff caveats

- The current README describes the fifteen-room campaign and the separate five-room mechanics circuit. Older M1 comments still appear in source; the parry window is 100 ms. Treat implementation and current tests as behavior, not the historical milestone labels.
- `ENEMIES.md` is referenced in `scripts/auditioner.gd` and `scripts/room_verse.gd`, but it was not included in the transferred archive. Do not invent rules attributed to it; recover it from the former workspace or remote if it exists.
- The initial archive lacked prior workspace history, license information, and export settings. Git history is now available in this repository; font licences ship with the fonts. There is still no configured distributable export.
- Godot 4.7.1 command-line exits can warn about one leaked `AudioStreamWAV` and one `AudioStreamPlaybackWAV` while the crackle loop is active. This matches confirmed upstream issue [godotengine/godot#76745](https://github.com/godotengine/godot/issues/76745) and does not fail the smoke suite.

## Regression-sensitive behavior

- The muted dummy never builds resonance. Its third successful parry emits `bout_won` without also emitting `shattered`.
- Player pogo reach intentionally matches enemy strike-hit reach at 120 px, so every enemy rebound is also a confirmed hit. Grounded foe strikes preserve footing; airborne strikes, including a jump in the same physics tick, can rebound from a vulnerable foe. Muted Test Pressings/HUSH are not pogoable from raw hits. Grooves retain launch priority on the ground and in the air.
- Strike cooldown remains 200 ms and the parry window remains 100 ms. One fresh press in the final 90 ms of cooldown may queue a strike; holding does not repeat. Stamp the parry clock when the strike executes. Hood, Set, damage, menus, respawn, and room transitions clear pending strikes. Ground recovery lasts 100 ms at 80% movement acceleration.
- Skip owns a three-strike Tap/Sweep/Accent chain with a 650 ms window between executed strikes. Queued input never advances it; public strike cancellation also clears the chain. Accent sets the existing `big` hit flag after launch resolution, so it neither stacks with a hot groove nor changes movement, reach, breath refill, or parry timing. `_strike_combo` is a separate captured presentation input; animation never drives combo state. `combo_readout.gd` only renders Main-supplied snapshots, with no independent gameplay clock.
- Title's Move practice creates `room_move_practice.gd`, an empty floor with side walls and no campaign/atlas registration. Main's `practice_mode` swaps in disposable progression, economy, map, pressing, and encounter models while keeping `_has_session` false. Exiting restores the original model objects before Continue/New Game; practice cannot save, record visits, or route into the campaign. Its pause menu says Return to title/Quit without implying a save. R clears position and chain; fresh entry suppresses the title's confirming input.
- The immediate 120 px radial strike impression and fainter groove/air echoes are presentation only, reink on the B-side, and add no hitstop or combat delay. Smoke and attack-feel regressions preserve range, outcome, airborne/grounded behavior, input buffering, cancellation, and parry timing.
- Progression regressions cover idempotent unlocks, snapshot restore, Gather's one dry breath, environmental capacity preservation, pickup persistence, and room recreation. Keep the distinction between environmental air and Gather when extending Refrains.
- Routing regressions cover the five-room bidirectional loop, Gather gate, named-entry respawns, transition ownership, world-map overlap/connectivity, route schema, dead-end budget, and stratum packing density.
- Inventory regressions cover full-screen anchoring, truthful slot counts, hidden names, live/silent refresh, unique input bindings, pause restoration, transition exclusion, gameplay suppression, and room-to-room persistence.
- B-side regressions cover the A-side presenting rooms exactly as authored, density and breaths inverting on the far face, a nearly spent side refusing to be turned, a spent side lifting the needle once by itself, grooves leaving and rejoining `live_groove` per side, ink and paper trading places and back, the burnished floor ringing only on its far face, and Main's flip staying inert without the Jump-Cut.
- Grayed-in world regressions cover the loader's contact model (one route per neighbour, canonical sides, real shared edges, one-way asymmetry, no id shadowing), every planned room booting with passages matching the plan and arrivals resolving on the far side, Refrain seals present and technique requirements never sealing, plinths clear of every arrival, climbs reachable on legs alone, and a Headshell walk that still reaches all 53 rooms and earns every Refrain.
- Save regressions cover schema/type/range validation, silent progression restoration, staged writes, backup recovery, and failure-safe handling. Campaign regressions cover the authored route, supported arrivals, reachable return paths, grounded endpoint interaction, persistent doors and encounter outcomes, title/Continue/pause ownership, and isolation from development mode. Keep those guarantees when adding rooms or checkpoint fields.
- Scenery regressions cover unchanged gameplay components, foreground clipping to platform faces, one atmosphere per room and cleanup on transitions, immediate camera synchronization on recovery, pause/reduced-motion behavior, A→B→A palette restoration, and unchanged development grayboxes. Native visual review still checks boss tells, traversal readability, and the full vertical Well.
- Lighting regressions cover authored profiles, native lights and inset platform occluders, one ambient modulator per room, fixed source positions, canvas-layer isolation, pause/reduced-motion behavior, live outcome energy, B-side exposure restoration, and unchanged development rooms. Native previews still verify visible shadows, soft edges, and print contrast.
